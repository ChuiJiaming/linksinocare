How we respond

We record receipt, proportionately verify identity and authority, and apply the relevant law. Deadlines and permitted extensions differ by jurisdiction; authentication is not universally the start date. Additional information is limited to what is necessary. Refusals include reasons and appeal or complaint routes.

Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing but may prevent future services that depend on the data. Deletion does not automatically cancel appointments or create a refund; service matters are addressed under the contract. We cannot unilaterally delete original records independently and lawfully retained by hospitals, but provide their contacts and relay requests as legally required.

If data must legally be retained, we specify the data, ground, period and restrictions. Requests are normally free; any lawful fee is explained in advance. We do not disclose another person’s records without valid authority.
